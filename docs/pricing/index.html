Pricing FAQ

Is the online converter really free?

Yes. The online tool is unlimited and requires no account. It is the same engine as every other version — free means free, with no watermark on the output.

Is the Pro license a subscription?

No. Desktop App Pro is a one-time payment of €39 (excl. VAT) for a lifetime license: 1 user, usable on up to 3 machines. There is no recurring charge.

Do API credits expire?

No. Purchased credits stay on your account until you use them. Every account also gets 10 free conversions every month, consumed before credits.

How are payments handled?

All payments go through Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record: card, Apple Pay or PayPal, VAT/sales tax calculated automatically, and an invoice sent by email.

Can I upgrade later?

Anytime. The free versions have no time limit, and Pro or API credits can be added whenever your needs grow — they all use the same conversion engine.
