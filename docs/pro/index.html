How the Hot Folder Works

Set it up once, then forget about it - every file you drop in is converted on its own.

  1. Choose your folders: pick the folder to watch and where the SVGs should go.
  2. Drop an image: save or copy any PNG, JPG, BMP or WebP file into the watch folder.
  3. It converts instantly: PixelToPath Pro detects the new file and converts it automatically.
  4. Collect your SVG: the finished, ready-to-use vector file is waiting in your output folder.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need PixelToPath Pro to convert images?

No. The free desktop app already converts PNG, JPG, BMP and WebP files to SVG, offline and without limits. PixelToPath Pro adds automation on top: hot folder monitoring, batch conversion of hundreds of images at once, and a command-line interface.

What exactly is a hot folder?

A hot folder is a folder that PixelToPath Pro keeps watching. Whenever a new image appears in it - because you saved, copied or dropped it there - the app detects it and converts it to SVG automatically, then places the result in the output folder you chose. You never have to open the app or click convert.

How many images can I convert at once with batch mode?

You can queue your entire library - import 100 images, or several hundred - and convert them all in one run. The only limit is your own machine, since every conversion runs locally on your CPU with the VTracer engine.

What can I do with the CLI?

The command-line interface lets you convert images with a single command, without opening the graphical interface. You can integrate vectorization into shell scripts, build pipelines and CI jobs, or schedule recurring conversions with tools like cron or Windows Task Scheduler.

Does the free version stay free?

Yes. The free desktop app remains free, unlimited and open source under the MIT license. PixelToPath Pro is an optional upgrade for professional workflows - the free app is not crippled or time-limited in any way.

Which operating systems does PixelToPath Pro support?

PixelToPath Pro runs on Windows (64-bit) and Linux, the same platforms as the free desktop app. Everything works fully offline, exactly like the free version.

Is the €39 price including taxes?

No - €39 is the price excluding VAT. Depending on your country and where you complete the purchase, VAT or sales tax may be added at checkout.

How many machines can I install PixelToPath Pro on?

The Pro license is a one-time purchase: €39 excl. VAT, yours for life. It covers a single user and can be activated on up to 3 machines - for example a desktop workstation, a laptop, and a studio computer.
